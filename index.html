jobsearch

A personal tool that runs on one person's own computer. It reads that person's own Gmail, read only, to confirm job applications they sent and to pick up sign-in codes from job sites.

Privacy policy

The tool has one user: the owner of the Google account that connects it. It asks Google for read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete mail.

Mail it reads stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

The owner can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
